# Swound

[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Website-swound.com-blue)](https://swound.com)
[![macOS](https://img.shields.io/badge/macOS-Supported-black?logo=apple)](https://swound.com)
[![Latest Release](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/swound/swound-desktop)](https://github.com/swound/swound-desktop/releases)

**Swound** is a collaborative cloud platform for music production, designed for modern producers, composers, beatmakers, and audio professionals.

Available for **macOS** and **Windows** (soon), Swound combines real-time cloud collaboration, intelligent project management, and access to the latest AI music generation models in a single desktop application.

Whether you're producing in **Ableton Live**, **Logic Pro**, **FL Studio**, or **GarageBand**, Swound makes it easy to collaborate with other musicians, organize projects, generate new musical ideas with AI, and keep your creative workflow synchronized across devices.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### ☁️ Cloud Collaboration for Professional DAWs

Unlike traditional cloud storage, Swound is built specifically for music production workflows.

Collaborate seamlessly on projects created in:

- Ableton Live
- Logic Pro
- FL Studio
- GarageBand

Swound understands the complexity of music production projects and provides a centralized workspace where producers can:

- Synchronize complete DAW projects
- Share sessions with collaborators
- Organize versions and revisions
- Manage stems, samples and project assets
- Keep projects safely backed up in the cloud
- Work across multiple computers

Whether you're producing remotely with another artist or moving between your studio and laptop, Swound keeps your entire production workflow synchronized.
Large sessions containing hundreds of audio files, MIDI clips, presets and stems remain organized inside a single cloud workspace.

## 🎵 AI Music and SFX Generation

Swound gives you access to multiple leading AI music models from a single platform. Choose the best model for each task or combine them in powerful AI workflows.

Generate:

- Full songs
- Instrumentals
- Samples and loops
- Melodies and chord progressions
- Drum patterns and bass lines
- Vocals
- Alternate versions and remixes

Instead of being locked to a single AI provider, Swound lets you combine the strengths of multiple leading AI models—including **Lyria Pro**, **ElevenLabs**, **Suno**, and more—within a single creative workflow. For example, you can generate a song with one model, create variations with another, extract stems, generate matching samples or loops, and continue or remix the result—all without leaving the application.

# Why Swound?

Swound is more than an AI music generator or a cloud storage service—it's a cloud platform built specifically for modern music production.

Unlike traditional cloud storage, Swound is designed to work seamlessly with professional DAW projects, making it easy for producers, composers, and teams to collaborate, synchronize projects, and manage every asset from a single workspace.

At the same time, Swound gives you access to multiple leading AI music models and production tools without interrupting your creative workflow.

With Swound, you can:

- Collaborate on Ableton Live, Logic Pro, FL Studio, and GarageBand projects
- Synchronize complete DAW projects and assets in the cloud
- Share sessions with collaborators from a centralized workspace
- Access multiple leading AI music generation models
- Generate full songs, instrumentals, samples, and loops
- Convert audio to high-accuracy multi-track MIDI
- Split songs into editable stems
- Organize projects, samples, presets, and production libraries
- Create, collaborate, and produce from anywhere
